# Privacy Policy — Vision for a Better Life SLA

*Effective Date: October 2026*  
*Applicable Jurisdiction: Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia*  
*Compliance: Personal Data Protection Proclamation (PDPP) & NBE Financial Consumer Protection Directives*  

---

## 1. Introduction
Vision for a Better Life Savings and Credit Cooperative Society ("Vision for a Better Life SLA", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and financial integrity of our members. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, secure, and retain your information when you use our mobile application and digital financial services.

## 2. Information We Collect
1. **Identification Data:** Name, phone number, date of birth, gender, address, and official national identification number (Fayda / Kebele ID / Passport).
2. **KYC Documents:** Photographic capture of your government-issued ID card and facial verification photograph (selfie).
3. **Financial Records:** Savings account balances, deposit transaction receipts, loan schedules, repayments, and double-entry ledger entries.
4. **Device & Security Telemetry:** Device model, Android OS version, and Google Play Integrity verdicts to protect against fraud and unauthorized account takeovers.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- To open and administer your member savings and credit cooperative accounts.
- To verify deposit payments and credit repayments through authorized banking switches (including verify.et API).
- To assess creditworthiness and disburse cooperative loans.
- To satisfy regulatory compliance obligations mandated by Ethiopian financial authorities.

## 4. Data Security & Storage
- **In Transit:** All communications use TLS 1.3 encryption with cryptographic certificate pinning (`network_security_config.xml`).
- **At Rest:** Database records and mobile SQLite databases are encrypted using AES-256 (SQLCipher with Android Keystore StrongBox).
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial information.

## 5. Member Rights & Account Deletion (S-507)
You retain the right to:
1. Access and export your financial transaction statement at any time via the application.
2. Update and correct your personal profile information.
3. **Request Account Deletion & Data Erasure:** You can request account deletion directly within the mobile application settings or by contacting our data protection officer. Upon deletion, all personal identification records and authentication credentials will be permanently erased. Non-personal double-entry financial ledger records are retained in compliance with statutory 7-year cooperative audit requirements.
